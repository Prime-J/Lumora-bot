🥋 Fighting Styles

Fighting styles are combat disciplines gained through Scrolls → Secret Codes → Trials. Each style has 3 moves with different power, accuracy, and energy costs.

How to Obtain a Style

  1. Find a Scroll — scrolls drop while hunting (not certain): 2.5% Common, 1% Rare, 0.3% Epic, 0.1% Legendary per hunt. New hunters receive a starter scroll at registration.
  2. Open it in your DM — the scroll reveals the quest briefing and your personal secret code.
  3. Speak the code in any group chat — one-time, personal to you, never share it. Your trial begins: you are teleported to a foreign land.
  4. PROCEED through the story (or RETREAT — the trial stays open and your code resumes it) and defeat the challenger NPC.
  5. Pay the teacher — bring the required materials (.sacrifice to hand them in) and the Lucon fee. Then the style is yours. Equip with .equip-style <name>.
🔑 Secret Codes

Every player gets a unique one-time code per style at registration. Codes are revealed only when you open the scroll in your DM. A spent code dissolves — the path it opened is yours alone. If you retreat mid-trial, speak the code again to resume where you stood.

⚡ Move Structure

Move 1 (Basic) — Low energy, high accuracy, moderate power
Move 2 (Utility) — Often 100% accuracy, may heal/buff/drain
Move 3 (Finisher) — High power, lower accuracy, high energy cost

Rarer styles hit harder (rarity bonus in combat) and carry harder gates: rarer scrolls, higher levels, pricier teachers.

⚡ Combat System

Wild Battles

When you .hunt, you may encounter wild Mora. You can .attack, .capture, or .flee. Wild Mora have varying levels based on terrain difficulty.

NPC Arena

Face named NPCs with unique fighting styles. Each difficulty tier has different NPCs:

TierNPCsFighting StyleDrop Chance
🟢 NoviceMira, Tolo, KadeWind Step, Sun Walk, Pyrolexis15-25%
🟡 WarriorLyra, Sereth, VaanTide Veil, Frost Mantle, Void Sever25-40%

PvP Battles

Challenge other players with .battle @player. PvP awards Glory and faction points but does NOT award DΞP. Losers lose a portion of their Honor/Bounty/Resonance.

Faction Wars

Periodic faction vs faction battles. Earn Glory for your faction. Top faction controls the Rift for a week and earns exclusive rewards.

💰 Economy

Currency: Lucons

Lucons are the primary currency. Earn them through hunting, daily rewards, bounties, and trading. Spend them at the market, faction market, and on services.

Earning Lucons

  • .daily — Daily login reward (scales with level)
  • .weekly — Weekly bonus
  • .hunt — Mora encounters drop Lucons
  • .bounty — Complete bounty quests for big payouts
  • .market sell — Sell items to other players

Spending Lucons

  • .market — Buy consumables, gear, scrolls
  • .f-market — Faction-exclusive items
  • .heal — Pay for healing at the Sanctuary
  • .travel — Energy cost for teleportation

Faction Points

Earned through raids, missions, and faction wars. Used at the Faction Market for exclusive gear and scrolls.

📈 Progression

DΞP (Experience)

DΞP is earned through Hunting Grounds and NPC Arena combat. PvP does NOT award DΞP. XP requirements scale exponentially:

LevelDΞP RequiredSource
1 → 2~100Hunting Wilderness Easy
5 → 6~500Hunting Standard + NPC Arena
10 → 11~1,500Dangerous terrains
20 → 21~5,000Shadow Hollow + Elite NPCs
50 → 51~25,000Rift Scar Nightmare + Mythic NPCs
⚠️ Anti-Farming

Fighting enemies far below your level gives reduced DΞP. Fighting enemies above your level gives an underdog bonus. Always push to harder content.

Stats

Players invest stat points into 4 attributes:

  • Strength — +Physical Damage, +Critical Chance
  • Agility — +Speed, +Evasion, +First Strike
  • Vitality — +Max HP, +HP Regen, +Defense
  • Mora Power — +Mora Damage, +Capture Rate, +Bond Strength

Honor / Bounty / Resonance

  • Honor — Earned through honorable combat and quests. Lost on death.
  • Bounty — Earned through bounty quests. Attracts NPC hunters.
  • Resonance — Earned through Mora bonding and Rift events. Controls Mora power.
